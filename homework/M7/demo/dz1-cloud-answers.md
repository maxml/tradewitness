In trading, the risk/reward ratio is a measure used to compare the potential risk of a trade to its potential reward. It helps traders evaluate the attractiveness of a trade based on how much they stand to gain relative to how much they could lose.

- **Risk**: The amount of capital you are willing to lose on a trade if it goes against you.
- **Reward**: The potential profit you expect to make if the trade goes in your favor.

A common way to express this ratio is as risk:reward (e.g., 1:2), indicating that for every unit of risk, there's a potential reward of two units. A favorable risk/reward ratio typically means that the potential reward outweighs the potential risk, which can lead to a more favorable trading strategy.
